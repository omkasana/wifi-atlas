## Automatic commercial license

Written approval is **not required**. Before commercial use, email **omkasana28@gmail.com** with your legal/business name, contact information, product/service, and version or commit used. This is accounting registration and a request for payment instructions, not discretionary approval.

The royalty is **25% of gross revenue attributable to the licensed app**, before expenses, marketplace commissions, or payment-processing fees. Separately collected remittance taxes and actual customer refunds are handled as defined in LICENSE. Unrelated business revenue is excluded; bundled products require a reasonable, documented allocation.

For example, with gross attributable revenue of 1,000 in a reporting currency and no exclusions, the royalty is 250 in that currency, even if development or marketplace expenses reduced your profit. No equity or ownership in your company is created.

## Quarterly reports and payment

Reports and payments are due **within 30 calendar days after each calendar quarter ends**:

- January–March: quarter ends March 31.
- April–June: quarter ends June 30.
- July–September: quarter ends September 30.
- October–December: quarter ends December 31.

Send the report to **omkasana28@gmail.com**, including the period, product, currency, revenue calculation, applicable exclusions/allocation, royalty, and payment reference. Commercial licensees report zero-revenue quarters as well.

Ask the same contact for usable payment instructions. The email itself is not advertised as a payment account. If instructions have not been provided by the deadline, submit the report and a documented offer to pay on time, reserve the amount due, and pay within 30 days of receiving usable instructions. That delay alone does not invalidate an otherwise compliant automatic license.
